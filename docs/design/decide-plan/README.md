# Decide's plan area: concepts and screenshots

David (30 Oct 2026, via Goldy): "Please make the planny area of the Decisions page more intersting looking. Feel free to hit up Gemini or OpenAI for design ideas if helpful".

**Concepts** (`concept-*.jpg`): made by Orbit in David's Tripo account with Tripo Studio's image generator, Nano Banana 2 (Gemini), at 16:9, one image each, on the free allowance (no credits). The reference image was the Decide backdrop (`research/station-assets/backdrop/room.jpg`). Each prompt was Chart's description, prefixed "Same room, same palette as the reference image. A concept for a game screen's decision panel, seen straight on and filling most of the frame:". It was followed by the palette, the accents (#6b5cff, #ff7a90), "Polished, like a modern dark dashboard", "no legible text, no letters, no numbers, no logos" and "No people, no plants, no warmth". The images are saved here as JPEG; nothing from them ships in the game.

- `concept-requisition-form.jpg`: **built**. Its three ruled boxes take the moves' texts as they are, and a form the bosses issue suits their voice.
- `concept-switch-console.jpg`: the label plates are too small for the moves' descriptions. One small "ON" label slipped in.
- `concept-punch-card-ledger.jpg`: the tall cards would push the moves below the first screen, and they came out a warm manila.

**Screenshots** (Chromium, 2D scene, production build): `before-1440.jpg` (main before this change), `after-1440.jpg`, `after-1280x720.jpg` (the heading starts on the first screen) and `after-two-chosen.jpg` (two moves stamped).
